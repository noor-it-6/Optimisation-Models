How to use these pages

  • Study mode shows every stage with notes, predict-first questions and challenges. Presenter mode shows one stage at a time; use the arrow keys or Back and Next.
  • Under each equation, open Example to see it written out in full with the optimal numbers plugged in.
  • Sliders re-solve the model in your browser, so you can explore what-if questions alongside the Excel files.
